## Task A · Ingresso «valido fino a» (good-till-time)
`place_order` and `place_bracket_order` (MCP + engine + admin manual order form) accept `valid_until` = ISO datetime or the keyword `"close"` (today's regular session close of the instrument's market; for crypto: not allowed → INVALID_ORDER with field). Only for the ENTRY: when unfilled at `valid_until`, the entry becomes `expired` and its bracket children (still held) are canceled; a partially filled entry expires its remainder and the children keep protecting the filled qty (reduced to the filled qty if the engine supports it; otherwise document current behaviour and test it). Children keep their own tif (gtc for swing). Live/broker-routed orders: SMTrading cancels the entry itself at `valid_until` through the normal cancel path (the processor job), never through a new broker endpoint. Order DTOs show `valid_until`. Telegram: an expired entry produces the existing «order.expired» activity. Agent docs: PM proposals and ES execution use `valid_until: "close"` for entries prepared in the pre-close windows (instead of «ANNULLA» at 22:15, which stays as fallback); RM checks it. HANDOFFS.md updated.
